      Privacy Policy

      Wispaway is built to need as little from you as possible. Here is exactly what that means.

      Who is responsible

      Wispaway is published by Logic Arc Studios (“we”). For anything about privacy, contact us at Poonamanime5509@gmail.com.

      What we collect: nothing

      We do not collect, store, share or sell personal information. The game has:

      • no accounts and no sign-in;
      • no analytics, tracking pixels or session recording;
      • no advertising code of our own;
      • no server of ours that receives anything you do in the game. The only outside request the game makes is to load the confetti effect (see “Third-party code” below).

      The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.

      What is stored on your device

      To remember where you left off, the game saves a small amount of data in your browser’s local storage. This data stays on your device. We never see it and it is never sent to us.

      What is savedWhy
      Your current level and the levels you have unlockedSo you can continue where you stopped and your progress carries over between visits.
      The puzzle you are in the middle ofSo an unfinished level can be picked up again.
      Difficulty modeSo the game opens in the mode you chose.
      Settings: sound, volume, vibration, effects, speed and light/dark themeSo the game feels the way you set it.
      Whether you have played beforeSo the start screen can show New Game or Continue.
      “My Levels”: levels you made from picturesSo you can replay them later. Only a simplified black-and-white grid of the shape is saved, plus the name and difficulty you chose, not the picture itself.

      Removing it. The game’s Settings has a reset option that sends your levels back to the start (your saved picture levels are kept). To delete everything, including “My Levels”, clear site data for the game in your browser settings. Using private or incognito mode means nothing is kept after you close the window.

      Pictures you choose

      If you make a level from a picture, the image is processed inside your browser only. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. When you choose a picture, your browser shows its normal file picker, and the game receives only the file you pick.

      The game turns the picture into a simplified black-and-white shape. If you save that level to “My Levels”, only the shape is stored on your device, not your original photo.

      Please read the Terms about pictures to see what is and is not appropriate to use.

      Sound and voice

      Game sounds are generated in code on your device, and nothing is recorded. The game may use your device’s vibration feature on supported phones. You can turn sound and vibration off in the settings.

      If a future version uses your browser’s built-in text-to-speech to read text aloud, note that, depending on your browser and device, the browser itself may use an online voice service. That is controlled by your browser or operating system, not by us.
